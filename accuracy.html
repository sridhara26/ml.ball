What log loss means

Log loss grades the probabilities, not just the picks. Every prediction is scored on the probability placed on the team that actually won: a right call costs a little, a wrong one costs more, and a confidently wrong one costs the most. Lower is better: 0.693 is pure coin flips and the high .60s is where solid MLB models land. A useful model lives below the coin-flip line.

Accuracy by confidence
